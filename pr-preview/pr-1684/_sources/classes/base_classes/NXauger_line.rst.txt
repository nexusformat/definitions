============
NXauger_line
============

**Status**:

  base class, extends :ref:`NXobject`

**Description**:

  .. collapse:: An Auger line. ...

      An Auger line.

      An Auger line comes from the non-radiative decay of a level with a core
      vacancy. A less tightly bound electron fills the vacancy and the atom
      emits a second electron, the Auger electron. The kinetic energy of the Auger
      electron is characteristic of the element and can depend on the chemical 
      state of the atom.

  **name**: (optional) :ref:`NX_CHAR <NX_CHAR>` 

    .. collapse:: Name of the Auger line. ...

        Name of the Auger line.

        The `IUPAC notation`_ has the form ``INITIAL-FINAL1FINAL2``.
        ``INITIAL`` identifies the initial level. ``FINAL1FINAL2`` identifies
        the final level, which contains two vacancies. For example, ``K-L1L2``
        denotes a transition from the K level to the L1L2 level, with vacancies
        in L1 and L2.

        .. _IUPAC notation: https://doi.org/10.1002/xrs.1300200308
