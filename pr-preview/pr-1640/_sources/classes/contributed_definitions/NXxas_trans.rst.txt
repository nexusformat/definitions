===========
NXxas_trans
===========

**Status**:

  *application definition* (contribution), extends :ref:`NXxas`

**Description**:

  .. collapse:: Application definition for X-ray absorption spectroscopy in transmission ...

      Application definition for X-ray absorption spectroscopy in transmission
      geometry.

      The Beer-Lambert law relates the absorbance, :math:`\mu(E)t`, to the
      intensities of the incident and transmitted beams:

      .. math:: \mu(E)t = -\ln(I/I_0)

      where :math:`\mu(E)` is the absorption coefficient at the photon energy
      :math:`E`, and :math:`t` is the sample thickness, :math:`I_0` is the
      intensity of the incident beam and :math:`I` is the intensity of the
      transmitted beam.
