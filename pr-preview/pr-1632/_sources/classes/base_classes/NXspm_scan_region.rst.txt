=================
NXspm_scan_region
=================

**Status**:

  base class, extends :ref:`NXobject`

**Description**:

  .. collapse:: The scan region is the area of phase space or sub-phase space where the scan is ...

      The scan region is the area of phase space or sub-phase space where the scan is
      performed. The region could be N-dimensional and is defined by the minimum and
      maximum values of the scan axes.

      Along each independent scan axis AXIS, the scan region is described either by the
      offset :math:`o` (``scan_offset_valueAXIS``) and the range :math:`r` (``scan_rangeAXIS``),
      or by the start :math:`s` (``scan_startAXIS``) and the end :math:`e` (``scan_endAXIS``).
      Either pair fully defines the scan region along that axis, with :math:`r = e - s`.
      Depending on the instrument, the offset refers either to the centre of the scan region
      or to its bottom-left corner (the point where the scan starts along each axis):

      * centre offset: :math:`s = o - r/2` and :math:`e = o + r/2`.
      * corner offset: :math:`s = o` and :math:`e = o + r`; the centre of the scan region is
        at :math:`o + r/2`.

      These relations hold along each axis of the scan frame, i.e. before the rotation by
      ``scan_angleAXIS`` is applied. In SPM, the centre-offset convention is the more common
      one.

  **scan_angle**\ :bolditalic:`AXIS`: (optional) :ref:`NX_NUMBER <NX_NUMBER>` {units=\ :ref:`NX_ANGLE <NX_ANGLE>`} 

    .. collapse:: The in-plane orientation of the scan region: the angle of the fast scan axis f ...

        The in-plane orientation of the scan region: the angle of the fast scan axis from the
        ``x`` axis of the scanner frame, as a rotation about ``z`` through the centre of the
        scan region. Positive angles follow the right-hand rule, i.e. they are counter-clockwise
        when viewed from the probe side looking down onto the sample surface.

        AXIS denotes a lateral scan axis (e.g. ``scan_angle_x``). Both lateral scan axes are
        rotated by the same angle, so all instances must have the same value, and
        ``scan_angle_x`` alone is sufficient.
