==================
NXspm_piezo_sensor
==================

**Status**:

  base class, extends :ref:`NXsensor`

**Description**:

  .. collapse:: A position sensor of the piezo scanner. It reports the position of the tip relat ...

      A position sensor of the piezo scanner. It reports the position of the tip relative to the
      sample, as given by the extension of the scanner piezos, along the axes of the frame named
      by ``depends_on`` (the :ref:`scanner_frame </NXspm/ENTRY/scanner_frame-group>`
      in NXspm). A group may describe all three axes or a single axis (e.g. the height, or Z,
      piezo sensor).
