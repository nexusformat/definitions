NXspm
=====

**Status**:

**Description**:

  .. collapse:: Scanning Probe Microscopy (SPM) is a branch of microscopy that utilizes a physic ...

      Scanning Probe Microscopy (SPM) is a branch of microscopy that utilizes a physical probe to scan the surface of
      sample and image it at the atomic level.

      The application class NXspm is designed as a skeleton and contains common technical concepts
      for specific SPM sub-techniques such as STM, STS, AFM etc. In addition, it can be utilized to
      describe the SPM experiments without further specialization for each sub-technique.

      **Frames of reference**

      SPM has no beam, so the default NeXus (McStas) coordinate system does not apply. NXspm
      uses five right-handed Cartesian frames. The **scanner frame** is the central frame:
      every other frame is defined relative to it, directly or through one intermediate frame.

      * **Scanner frame** (central): origin at the undeflected piezo position (zero offset,
        zero scan angle, Z piezo at its zero) or at the centre determined by the scanner
        calibration. ``x`` and ``y`` are the lateral drive axes of
        the scanner; ``z`` is normal to the sample plane and points from the sample towards
        the probe. It describes the relative tip-sample displacement, independent of whether
        the tip or the sample is moved.
      * **Scan frame** (one per scan): origin at the centre of the scan area, ``x`` along the
        fast scan axis, ``y`` along the slow scan axis. It is placed in the scanner frame by
        the scan offset and the scan angle.
      * **Probe frame** (AFM): origin at the clamped end of the cantilever on the chip, ``x``
        along the cantilever long axis from the chip towards the tip, ``z`` normal to the
        cantilever. It is oriented in the scanner frame by the cantilever tilt and its
        in-plane angle.
      * **Stage frame**: the sample stage or coarse positioner, including the sample holder.
        It is placed in the scanner frame by the stage position. If no stage position is
        recorded, it coincides with the scanner frame.
      * **Sample frame**: origin at a fiducial on the sample, ``z`` normal to the sample
        surface. It is placed in the stage frame by the mounting translation and rotation.

      The relationships follow the ``depends_on`` direction of :ref:`NXtransformations`, which
      points towards the scanner frame:

      .. code-block:: text

          scan frame   --[offset, scan angle]---------+
          probe frame  --[tilt, in-plane angle]-------+--> scanner frame
          stage frame  --[stage position]-------------+
          sample frame --[mount translation, rotation]--> stage frame

      Conventions: positive rotations about ``z`` follow the right-hand rule, i.e. they are
      counter-clockwise when viewed from the probe side looking down onto the sample surface
      (from ``+z`` towards ``-z``). The measured data stay on the grid of the scan
      frame and are not resampled; only the transformations are recorded.
