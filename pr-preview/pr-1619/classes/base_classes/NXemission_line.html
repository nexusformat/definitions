3.3.1.91. NXemission_line¶

Status:

base class, extends NXobject

Description:

A characteristic X-ray emission line, a group of unresolved emission lines, …

A characteristic X-ray emission line, a group of unresolved emission lines, or an emission band.

An emission line comes from the radiative decay of a level with a core vacancy. A less tightly bound electron fills the vacancy and the atom emits a photon. The photon energy is characteristic of the element and can depend on the chemical state of the atom.

Symbols:

No symbol table

Groups cited:

NXelement

Structure:

name: (optional) NX_CHAR

Name of the emission line, group of unresolved lines, or band, in IUPAC …

Name of the emission line, group of unresolved lines, or band, in IUPAC or Latinized Siegbahn notation.

The IUPAC notation has the form INITIAL-FINAL. INITIAL identifies the initial level. FINAL identifies the final level. For example, K-L3 denotes a transition from the K level to the L3 level.

In the IUPAC notation, a comma joins the two levels of a spin-orbit doublet when the measurement does not resolve them (e.g. K-L2,3 for K-L2 and K-L3). A level symbol without a number (e.g. O in K-O) means that the number is unknown or irrelevant. V denotes a level with a valence-electron vacancy (e.g. K-V).

The name can also use Latinized Siegbahn notation (e.g. Ka1). This definition specifies the ASCII spellings in the table below. Prefer the IUPAC notation.

In the table below, three IUPAC names have two Siegbahn names: K-N4 (Kb4II and Kb4x), L3-N6,7 (Lb7' and Lu) and L2-N6(7) (Lg8' and Lv).
